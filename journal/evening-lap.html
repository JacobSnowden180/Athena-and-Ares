Trail log · Trail note

The evening lap.

The short loop that keeps the promise. No street names. The longer nights are the same dogs, given more room.

I keep the day honest with a short loop. About half a mile from the apartment, out and back, with a stop in a grassy field so they can sniff and take care of business. I think of it as the apartment lap. It is not glamorous, and it is the reason the bigger runs exist.

Athena is a lazy bones until the harness is on. Then she is the big mama, and she pulls. Ares is the goofball, physical about it, the one who will put his whole weight into you, and still a good boy who knows his place. He has calmed down some. I do not need him quiet.

We do not take the same kind of night every time. Some nights stay on that half mile. Some stretch to a couple of miles. The easy neighborhood distance is about five, if we rest and drink. I set the pace for wherever we are. I have had us around seven or eight miles an hour when the way is open. I do not chase the number.

On the hot nights the boots go on. I have packed four cold bottles for four miles when the air was still in the eighties. We skip the pavement if rain is still sitting on it. We wait about two hours after a big meal, because of bloat. Those rules are how we get to go again tomorrow.

It started when I brought home an electric scooter. It took a long time before this felt like ours. We have gone every day since. The lap is the promise. The longer runs are what we get on top of keeping it.

Athena takes the front seat of the Jeep when we are driving, because she decided that. Ares takes the back. On the scooter the seats do not matter. She is on my left, and he is on my right, and the cues are the same ones they already know.
