About

Why I do this.

I write this site. Athena and Ares are my dogs, and urban mushing is the thing we do.

I wanted them to have real exercise. A walk that barely leaves the building was never going to be enough for a Labradane and a Pyrenees mix, and I did not want their day to be a chore I resented. The scooter made it play.

It is also, I have noticed, play for other people. A black dog and a cream dog, side by side, with a plain kick scooter in Austin at night, tends to make a person smile. I like that. I think of it as serving the good. The dogs get the work their bodies want, and the street gets a small happy scene instead of nothing.

Athena picks the front seat of the Jeep. Ares takes the back. On the scooter she is on my left and he is on my right. She is shy until it is time. He is a storm with manners. Both of them are healthy. We keep up with the vet, and they stay on a preventative.

I am not selling anything here. I would like to become someone who can teach this clearly. The urban mushing page is the first set of notes. The trail log is where the days will go, when I have a day worth writing without inventing a route to make it sound bigger.

The scooter is a GoTrax XR Pro. It took a long time after I got it. We have gone every day since, even when the outing is only the half-mile lap and the grassy stop. That is enough. The five-mile nights are a gift on top of the habit.

If you have seen us, I hope it was a good moment in your evening. If you want to write, the contact page has the email. Social accounts are not up yet. I will not pretend they are.
