Adventure map

A map that keeps its secrets.

Austin is the only real place on this page. The rest is a mythic sketch: olive shade for Athena’s side, ember ridge for Ares, a loop for the daily lap, a ring for the easy five miles. No building, no block, no turn-by-turn.

  1. The apartment loop

    About half a mile, most days. A sniff and a potty stop belong on it. The drawing is a small oval on purpose. It is not the shape of a real block.

  2. The grassy field

    The pause on the daily lap. Grass, noses, and the ordinary business of dogs. Not a named park, and not a pin you could drive to from this page.

  3. The five-mile ring

    The easy neighborhood distance, with rest and water. The ring is a symbol for “far enough,” not a measured course.

  4. Athena’s side

    She runs on the left. Olive shade, because she is the quiet one until she decides to pull. Cue: “Athena, Nina, Nina, Nina.”

  5. Ares’ side

    He runs on the right. Ember ridge, because he is the warm, physical one. Cue: “Ares, Ares, Ares.”

  6. Open trail

    A blank marker. I can rename it in the SVG and in this list when a night deserves a name. The real streets stay off the page.

  7. Another open trail

    A second placeholder, up in the star margin. I can decide what it means later. It does not mean an address.

There is no live map and no GPS here. If a photo in the gallery has a place sticker on it, that sticker is not repeated as a destination.
