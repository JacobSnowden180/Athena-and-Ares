Austin · urban mushing

Athena & Ares

Two dogs and one ordinary electric kick scooter. I call the practice urban mushing, or scooterjoring: real miles in the city, with Athena on my left, Ares on my right, and a careful hand on the throttle.

Meet them Read the method

Left

Athena

Labradane, about 5. The cue to go left is “Athena, Nina, Nina, Nina.” Quiet until it is time, and then she really pulls.

Right

Ares

Great Pyrenees and Anatolian Shepherd, about 4. The cue to go right is “Ares, Ares, Ares.” A physical goofball, and a good boy.

Plate 03 · The rig

Not a specialty dog scooter.

The scooter is a GoTrax XR Pro, the ordinary kind, about three hundred dollars. The harnesses are tactical, because this is city riding and I want the control in my hand. The lead is fixed-length. No retractables.
